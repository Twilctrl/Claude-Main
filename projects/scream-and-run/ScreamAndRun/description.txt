Scream and Run adds a horror survival event.

Craft a Love Letter and read it. Onryo, the transfer student, notices you. She cannot be hurt, and one touch is death. Survive until she gives up.

- Phase 1, Stalking: she wanders closer and pretends not to see you.
- Phase 2, Hunting: she follows where she last saw you. Break line of sight and she loses you.
- Phase 3, Frenzy: the final minute. She teleports behind you, and you'll hear it coming.

Hide in a Locker (right-click), crouch in the dark, and stay quiet. Mining, running and attacking all make noise.

Single-player only. Doesn't touch any other mod's content and works alongside Calamity.
